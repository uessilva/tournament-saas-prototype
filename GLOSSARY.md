# Glossary

## User

A registered platform account with base permissions. A User may acquire additional roles within an Event.

## Event Owner

A User who created an Event and has full administrative control over that Event, including its Competitions, Teams, Matches, and published results.

## Event

A container owned by an Event Owner for one or more Competitions.

## Competition

A sport-specific contest within an Event, configured with a tournament format, eligibility rules, participating Teams, Matches, and standings.

## Team Manager

A User invited to manage one or more Teams within a Competition, including their rosters. A Team Manager cannot administer the rest of the Event.

## Team

A Competition entrant managed by a Team Manager. In an individual sport, a Team may represent one Participant.

## Participant

A person recorded on a Team roster. A Participant does not need a platform User account.

## Match

A scheduled contest between Competition entrants whose confirmed result contributes to standings and statistics.

## Venue

An Event-scoped named location or playing area that may be assigned to a Match.

## Bye

An automatic advancement granted to a Competition entrant when a knockout bracket has fewer entrants than available starting positions.

## Walkover

A Match outcome in which one Competition entrant advances or wins because the opponent did not or could not compete.

## Scoresheet

A standardized, competition-specific PDF completed during a Match and uploaded for result and statistics extraction. Extracted values require human confirmation before publication.
