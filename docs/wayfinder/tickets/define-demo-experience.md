# Define the guided demo experience

## Question

What navigation, screens, seeded scenario, and visual fidelity should demonstrate the complete Event Owner, Team Manager, and public-audience journeys? Produce a cheap, concrete flow or wireframe for the human to react to.

## Resolution

Use **Option C — Event hub**, a public-first editorial structure with polished styling, responsive layouts, realistic data, and no substantially additional screens. Retain the Owner setup, Team invitation, Team Manager roster, fixture/Scoresheet, reviewed-result, and public leaderboard views demonstrated in the wireframe.

Seed **Summer Cup 2026** with **Under-12 Football** as the guided end-to-end journey and **Tennis Open** as the secondary Competition. Keep all seeded people and contact details fictitious.

Ship the prototype on localhost first. As a follow-up, deploy it at a public URL so external visitors can follow published fixtures, results, standings, and statistic leaders without signing in. Keep Owner and Manager areas authenticated and demo reset controls protected.
